About Us

At Migrantplus, we are a leading cross-border mobility and legal advisory firm with an extensive presence across Latin America. We specialize in comprehensive immigration solutions, visa facilitation, and regulatory compliance for individuals, families, and businesses navigating global transitions.

Services

At Migrantplus, we provide comprehensive legal guidance and end-to-end representation for international mobility, specializing in consular processing and visa acquisition for the United States, Canada, and Mexico. Our regional experts ensure strict regulatory compliance and strategic preparation at every stage of your application.

Our practice covers a full spectrum of visa categories, tailored to your specific personal, professional, and medical needs:

  • Visitor & Tourism Visas (B1/B2, Temporary Resident Visas): Comprehensive application support for leisure travel, business exploratory trips, and short-term visits to the U.S., Canada, and Mexico.
  • Study & Academic Visas (F-1, M-1, Study Permits): Strategic advisory for international students enrolling in accredited educational institutions, language programs, and vocational training.
  • Family-Based & Marriage Visas: Legal facilitation for spouses, fiancés(es), and family-sponsored immigration pathways, ensuring proper evidentiary compliance for marital and dependent unions.
  • Family Reunification Programs: Specialized legal frameworks designed to reunite families across international borders through authorized sponsorship and relative petition channels.
  • Employment & Work Visas (Temporary Work Permits, Professional Visas): Representation for corporate transferees, skilled professionals, and sponsored employment classifications to secure lawful authorization to work abroad.
  • Medical Treatment & Childbirth Visas: Dedicated legal and advisory support for patients seeking specialized medical care, diagnostics, or surgical procedures abroad, alongside comprehensive visa assistance for planned childbirth and medical tourism in foreign clinics, including coordination with authorized healthcare providers.

Through meticulous document preparation, interview coaching, and adherence to immigration law, we mitigate compliance risks and maximize the probability of a favorable adjudication.
